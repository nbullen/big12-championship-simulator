Rules, sources & limitations

The two best conference winning percentages qualify. Ties proceed through head-to-head, common conference opponents, highest-placed common opponents, opponents’ combined conference winning percentage, eligible season wins, the final SportSource Analytics rating, then a coin toss.

Multi-team ties use the conference’s special head-to-head procedure. Once a berth is selected, the remaining tied teams restart. A tie reduced to two teams restarts the two-team procedure. Tied common-opponent standings bands are considered together.

Future SportSource ratings and an official coin toss cannot be known. If earlier steps do not settle a berth, this app shows possible pairs instead of inventing a winner. Eligible wins exclude exempt games and count no more than one FCS-or-lower win. The snapshot’s nonconference eligible-win counts are provisional: individual FCS qualification, waivers, and exempt-game status have not been independently certified. Any result reaching step E should be confirmed with the conference.

Policy caveat: the official live policy page is labeled 2025; its linked full procedures begin with the 2024 sixteen-team format. This app uses those latest published procedures, not an independently confirmed 2026 revision.

Snapshot data is embedded for offline use. It does not automatically update. Picks are saved only on this device when browser storage permits. Nothing is uploaded. This is an unofficial fan tool.
