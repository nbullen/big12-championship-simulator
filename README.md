# Big 12 Championship Simulator

Unofficial static football what-if app: https://nbullen.github.io/big12-championship-simulator/

Pick any Big 12 team, lock winners, and simulate the remaining conference schedule. Single-season and500-season runs use ESPN Matchup Predictor pregame win percentages for all24 remaining games, retrieved November10,2026 MST. Records are the November9 snapshot. Percentages are dated, not live. Independent draws do not update team strength after hypothetical results. Rounding-only100.1% pairs are explicitly normalized for drawing while the original source percentages remain displayed.

Includes all16 real team logos and ESPN metadata team colors, plus a logo-enabled dropdown. Team logos and trademarks belong to their respective owners; not affiliated with ESPN or the Big 12.

Your manually locked picks remain in your browser. No picks are uploaded. Official rule sources, model sources and limitations are linked inside the app.
